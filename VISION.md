# Vision

Make JPop's November 2026 Scottsdale birthday trip feel bigger, sharper, and
more inevitable than the hotel marketing. The site is a public-safe hype engine
and decision surface for friends—not a generic itinerary template.

## Product character

- Desert-after-dark luxury with heat, depth, motion, and a little menace.
- Irreverent copy for the actual audience. The babies cannot read it.
- Mobile-first, fast, image-rich, and playful without hiding useful logistics.
- Confirmed facts and compelling options are clearly distinguished.
- Sean appears as the lobster concierge and occasional bad influence.

## Privacy contract

Public: trip dates, broad lodging details, public venue information, options,
links, shared anonymous vote totals, and tentative itineraries.

Never public: traveler identities, room assignments, reservation numbers,
confirmation documents, contact information, private financial details, or live
locations.
